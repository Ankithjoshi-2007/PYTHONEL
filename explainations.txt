# 3D Aircraft Flight Performance Analyzer

This project is a high-fidelity Python-based aircraft performance simulator designed to model finite-wing 3D aerodynamics using realistic aerospace engineering equations. Unlike simple 2D airfoil calculators, this simulator applies finite-wing corrections, induced drag modeling, compressibility effects, Reynolds number effects, and stall behavior to better represent real aircraft flight conditions.

The simulator supports both:

* Direct aerodynamic coefficient input mode
* Physics-based NACA 4-digit airfoil simulation mode

The program analyzes aircraft aerodynamic behavior under varying flight conditions and generates a detailed engineering-style flight performance report.

---

* Finite-wing 3D aerodynamic modeling

# Major Aerodynamic Equations Used

## Wing Reference Area

S = b * c

where:

* b = wingspan
* c = mean aerodynamic chord

---

## Aspect Ratio

AR = b² / S

Aspect ratio is a key parameter controlling induced drag and aerodynamic efficiency.

---

## Dynamic Pressure

q = 0.5 * rho * V²

This represents the kinetic energy density of airflow.

---

## Reynolds Number

Re = (rho * V * c) / mu

Used to estimate turbulent boundary-layer effects and profile drag.

---

## Mach Number

M = V / a

Used for compressibility and wave-drag calculations.

---

## 2D Lift Coefficient

CL = CL_alpha * (alpha - alpha0)

Used in NACA-based aerodynamic solving.

---

## Finite-Wing 3D Lift Correction

CL_3D = CL_2D / (1 + (CL_2D / (pi * e * AR)))

Corrects 2D airfoil lift predictions for realistic 3D finite-wing behavior.

---

## Induced Drag

CDi = CL² / (pi * e * AR)

Models drag caused by wingtip vortices and lift generation.

---

## Total Drag Coefficient

CD = CD0 + CDi + CD_wave + CD_stall

Combines:

* parasite drag
* induced drag
* compressibility wave drag
* stall drag

---

## Lift Force

L = q * S * CL

---

## Drag Force

D = q * S * CD

---

## Power Required

P = D * V

Used to estimate engine power needed to maintain flight speed.

# Aerodynamic Modeling Logic

The simulator first computes the aircraft geometric properties such as wing area and aspect ratio. It then calculates flow properties including Reynolds number, Mach number, and dynamic pressure.

In NACA simulation mode, the program derives aerodynamic coefficients directly from airfoil geometry and angle of attack using thin-airfoil theory and aerodynamic correction models.

The solver then applies:

* finite-wing corrections
* induced drag models
* compressibility corrections
* wave drag estimation
* stall behavior modeling

Finally, aerodynamic forces and aircraft performance metrics are computed to determine overall flight state and aircraft behavior.

---

# Flight State Analysis

The simulator determines aircraft operating condition based on aerodynamic force balance:

* Stable Flight
* Climbing
* Descending
* Stall Warning
* Full Stall Condition

This is achieved using net vertical force calculations and stall-angle detection.
